Answers

Frequently asked questions.

Do these labels work with inkjet or laser printers?

No. These are direct thermal labels and are made for compatible direct thermal printers.

Are the labels perforated?

Yes. The fanfold stacks include pre-cut perforations for fast, clean tear-off during packing.

What surfaces do they stick to?

The permanent adhesive is designed for corrugated boxes, poly mailers, bubble mailers, and envelopes.

Which pack should I start with?

Start with 500 labels for lighter shipping, 2000 labels for steady store volume, or 3000 labels for high-volume FBA and warehouse use.
